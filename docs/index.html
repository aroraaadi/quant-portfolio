Methodology

Signals. Each name is scored cross-sectionally on four multi-metric signals: value (earnings yield + EV/EBITDA yield + FCF yield), quality (ROIC stability + operating margin + ROE + low leverage), 12−1 month momentum, and short interest (negated). Every metric is sector-neutralized before ranking, so the tilts are stock selection rather than sector bets. With a small universe, raw z-scores are outlier-dominated, so each signal uses a Gaussianized rank.

Risk. A statistical factor model Σ = BFBᵀ + D estimated on the trailing year: B holds the loadings of the top three principal components, D the idiosyncratic variances. A Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage estimate is computed alongside as a cross-check every run.

Construction. Weights maximize αᵀw − λ·wᵀΣw − TC(w, w₀), long-only, fully invested, 20% position cap. λ is calibrated so portfolio volatility lands in a 12–18% annualized band, and positions under 2% are pruned for concentration. Rebalanced monthly.

Personal research project, not investment advice. Benchmarks are price indices (no dividends); portfolio returns are also price-only and net of an assumed 10 bps per unit of rebalance turnover. Performance before the live date is hypothetical: it applies today's portfolio backwards and carries selection and lookahead bias.
