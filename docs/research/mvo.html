Educational tool, not investment advice. Covariance is a Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage estimate over the full return history; the frontier is long-only and fully invested. Expected returns are noisy estimates — small changes move the frontier a lot, which is exactly why concentrated MVO solutions should be treated with caution.
